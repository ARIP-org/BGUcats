Everyone knows the campus cats.
Nobody knows their names.

There are around fifty cats living on this campus. They have territories, routines and very strong opinions about the cafeteria. What they don't have is a record — so we're building one, and we need your photos to do it.

➤ Send a cat photo opens @bgucatsbot on Telegram

How it works

  1. 1

    See a cat

    Anywhere on campus. Sitting on a bench, asleep under a bush, walking into a building like it pays tuition.

  2. 2

    Send the photos

    Open the bot and send as many as you like. Several angles of the same cat are far more useful than one perfect shot.

  3. 3

    That's it

    We sort, group and name them. Every photo you send makes the next cat easier to recognise.

Why so many photos?

Because telling cats apart is genuinely hard — for people and for computers. Two different ginger tabbies look more alike to software than two photos of the same cat taken in different light. The only way past that is a lot of labelled examples of each individual cat.

So the aim is a proper photo library: many pictures of each cat, in different light, from different angles, taken by different people. Eventually you'll be able to point your phone at a cat and be told who it is. Until then, we're collecting.

What we keep

The photo, and roughly when it arrived. Your Telegram ID is hashed before it is stored, so submissions can be grouped without anyone being identifiable. We don't record where you were standing, and location data is stripped from every photo we publish.
