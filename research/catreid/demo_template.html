How it decides
1 · Overall look

A model trained to tell individual animals apart turns each photo into a fingerprint. Photos of the same animal should have similar fingerprints.

2 · Matching details

For the closest candidates, it pairs up small distinctive points between the two photos. Only points on the animal count — points on rocks or grass are ignored.

3 · Decide

Very sure: it answers. Unsure: it shows you its top 3 and you pick, or tap "none of these". Nothing close: it says this looks like a new animal.
